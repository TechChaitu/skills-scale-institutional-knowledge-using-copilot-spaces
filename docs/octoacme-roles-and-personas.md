# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Technical Lead / Architecture Lead

### Role Summary
Technical Leads provide architectural direction, technical decision-making, and engineering alignment across delivery teams. They help ensure the solution remains scalable, maintainable, and consistent with the product and platform strategy.

### Responsibilities
- Define or guide technical architecture and design decisions
- Align engineering implementation with product, platform, and integration needs
- Review dependencies and technical risks across workstreams
- Support developers in resolving design and implementation challenges
- Partner with the Product Manager and Project Manager on trade-offs affecting scope, quality, and delivery

### Goals
- Keep technical decisions aligned with business goals and platform constraints
- Reduce unnecessary rework and architecture drift
- Improve delivery confidence through clear technical guidance

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and architecture discussions
- Dependency and risk updates during planning and execution
- Coordination with developers, PMs, and stakeholders on technical trade-offs

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works with Developers to guide implementation and review technical quality
- Supports Project Managers by clarifying dependencies, sequencing, and risk areas
- Helps Product Managers evaluate feasibility and trade-offs against roadmap goals

---

## QA Lead / Test Manager

### Role Summary
QA Leads ensure product quality through validation planning, test strategy, and acceptance criteria coverage. They help the team ship with confidence by aligning testing with release readiness and customer expectations.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy and release validation plans
- Review acceptance criteria and quality gates
- Coordinate manual and automated testing efforts
- Identify gaps in coverage and raise quality risks early
- Partner with Developers and Product Managers to confirm readiness for release

### Goals
- Improve confidence in product quality and release readiness
- Catch defects earlier and reduce post-release issues
- Maintain clear, measurable validation standards

### Typical Communication
- Quality checks, release readiness reviews, and defect triage
- Feedback loops with developers and product stakeholders
- Validation status in sprint and milestone reviews

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works with Developers to validate implementation and resolve defects
- Supports Product Managers by confirming outcomes against acceptance criteria
- Coordinates with Project Managers on release readiness and quality risks

---

## Release Manager

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate the release process, deployment readiness, and communication before and after production changes. They provide visibility into sequencing, rollback readiness, and launch confidence.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate release windows, schedules, and dependencies
- Confirm pre-release checks, smoke tests, and rollback plans
- Communicate release status to stakeholders and support teams
- Track issues during production rollout and activation
- Support post-release verification and incident communication if necessary

### Goals
- Reduce release risk and improve rollout predictability
- Keep business stakeholders informed during high-impact changes
- Ensure clear ownership for production readiness and communication

### Typical Communication
- Release readiness reviews and deployment checklists
- Stakeholder updates before and after go-live
- Incident and rollback coordination if a release is unstable

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works with Project Managers to align release timing with milestones and dependencies
- Collaborates with Developers and QA Leads to ensure all release criteria are met
- Keeps Product Managers informed about release impact and known issues

---

## Security Champion / Compliance Partner

### Role Summary
Security Champions and Compliance Partners help protect the project from security and regulatory risk by identifying concerns early and ensuring policy alignment throughout delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Review design and implementation for security and compliance concerns
- Identify risk areas, control gaps, and sensitive data handling needs
- Support secure development practices and review security checklists
- Help coordinate escalation for incidents or high-risk findings
- Align work with organizational policies and regulatory requirements

### Goals
- Reduce risk of security issues and compliance violations
- Build security and privacy into the delivery process from the beginning
- Improve trust and resilience of output products and services

### Typical Communication
- Security reviews, risk assessments, and governance checkpoints
- Escalation updates for high-risk findings or issues
- Guidance for engineering, PM, and stakeholder teams on controls and timing

### Interaction with existing roles
- Advises Developers on secure design and implementation practices
- Helps Project Managers assess risk and dependency impacts
- Supports Product Managers in balancing delivery speed with required compliance standards

---

## Customer Success / Stakeholder Partner

### Role Summary
Customer Success or Stakeholder Partners represent the user and operational perspective, helping teams understand adoption, service expectations, and communications needs as work moves through the lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Gather stakeholder feedback and user experience insights
- Represent customer impact, support needs, and adoption concerns
- Help validate whether a release or milestone is ready for stakeholder use
- Coordinate customer-facing communications and feedback loops
- Support prioritization with real-world operational context

### Goals
- Improve customer value and adoption of delivered work
- Surface issues early and support smoother rollout experiences
- Ensure stakeholder feedback informs roadmap and delivery decisions

### Typical Communication
- Stakeholder updates, user feedback reviews, and release impact discussions
- Post-launch feedback and support readiness reviews
- Alignment with Product Managers and Project Managers on customer impact

### Interaction with existing roles
- Provides Product Managers with user and stakeholder context for prioritization
- Helps Project Managers identify communication needs and delivery dependencies
- Shares operational feedback with Developers and QA Leads to improve readiness and usability

---

## Team Facilitator / Scrum Master

### Role Summary
Team Facilitators help the team work effectively by supporting agile practices, removing blockers, improving collaboration, and creating a healthy team operating rhythm.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate planning, standups, retrospectives, and working sessions
- Help the team surface blockers and resolve process friction
- Improve team communication and decision-making quality
- Support continuous improvement and delivery flow
- Coordinate with Project Managers and Product Managers on team health and delivery cadence

### Goals
- Improve team effectiveness, focus, and collaboration
- Reduce unnecessary friction in execution and coordination
- Support consistent, sustainable delivery patterns

### Typical Communication
- Team ceremonies, backlog refinement, and retrospectives
- Coaching and facilitation discussions with product and engineering leads
- Alignment updates with PMs on delivery health and process improvements

### Interaction with existing roles
- Supports Developers by protecting focus and improving delivery flow
- Helps Project Managers maintain momentum and visibility across milestones
- Partners with Product Managers to align priorities, planning, and stakeholder communication

---
